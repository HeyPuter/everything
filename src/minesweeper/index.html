How to play
  • Left-click (or Enter / Space) reveals a cell. The first click is always safe.
  • Right-click, long-press, Shift+Enter, or F plants a flag.
  • Middle-click, both buttons, or C chords a numbered cell when its flags match.
  • Clear every non-mine cell to win. The face button starts a new game (R).
