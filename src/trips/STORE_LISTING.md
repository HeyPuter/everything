# Trips

**Plan trips together, keep the data yourself.**

## Short description

A collaborative trip planner with a day-by-day itinerary, a live map, bookings, shared costs, packing lists and chat. Each trip lives in its owner's own Puter cloud.

## Long description

Trips puts the whole journey in one place. Lay out each day with the places you want to see, drag stops between days, and watch the route draw itself on the map with travel times between them. Trips estimates when you will arrive at each stop, warns you when a museum is closed or a day runs too long, and can schedule a day for you around your fixed bookings.

Flights, trains, hotels, rental cars and restaurant reservations sit in the same timeline, in the right time zone and on the right day. Drop in a confirmation PDF, an email or a wallet pass and the AI importer turns it into a booking for you to review. Track expenses in any currency, split them evenly or by shares, and settle up with the fewest payments. Packing lists, to-dos, files, notes, polls and a group chat keep everyone on the same page.

Invite people with a link or by their Puter username, and give each one a role: editors plan with you, viewers follow along. Changes reach everyone automatically, and everything keeps working offline, then syncs when you reconnect. Share a read-only public link, subscribe to the trip in your calendar app, print it or save it as a PDF, and export it to GPX, KML or a trip file.

What makes Trips different is where your data lives. There is no shared Trips server. When you create your first trip, Trips sets up a small server in your own Puter account, and every trip you own lives there. Preferences, private expenses and private packing items stay in each person's own account. You can download all of your data, or delete it, at any time.

Beyond the itinerary, Trips keeps a map of the countries and regions you have visited, a bucket list, a travel journal with photos, a road trip planner with driving limits and fuel stops, shared collections of favourite places, and a leave planner for working out time off with friends and family.

## Key features

- **Plan day by day**, with drag and drop between days and the places pool, undo and redo, and a Plan B for any day.
- **See it on the map**, with routes per day, travel times, place search, nearby suggestions and one-click route optimization.
- **Keep every booking in one timeline**, from multi-leg flights to hotel stays, each in its own time zone, with optional live flight status.
- **Import bookings with AI**, from PDFs, emails, wallet passes or your Puter mailbox, and review every one before it is saved.
- **Split costs fairly**, in any currency with rates frozen at the time of the expense, then settle up with the fewest transfers.
- **Pack and prepare together**, with shared packing lists, bags, per-person checks, templates and to-dos.
- **Talk it through**, with chat, notes, polls, shared links and an activity feed of what changed.
- **Ask the assistant**, which reads the trip and proposes changes that you approve before anything is written.
- **Work offline**, with trips, files and map tiles kept on your device and edits that sync when you are back online.
- **Share on your terms**, with roles, a permission matrix, guests, a public read-only link and a calendar feed.
- **Own your data**, stored in your own Puter cloud, with a full export and a one-step delete.

## Who it's for

- Friends and families planning a holiday together.
- Couples who want one shared itinerary instead of a pile of confirmation emails.
- Road trippers who need driving times, overnight stops and fuel planning.
- Group organisers who track who paid for what and who owes whom.
- Frequent travellers who want a record of where they have been.
- Anyone who would rather keep their travel plans in storage they control.

## Highlights in this version

- Time-aware day planning with travel times, opening hours and an automatic day schedule.
- AI booking import and a trip assistant that always asks before changing anything.
- Offline trips with downloaded map tiles and files.
- Travel journal, atlas with regions and bucket list, road trip mode and collections of saved places.
- Background notifications by webhook, ntfy or email digest, and reminders for bookings and to-dos.
