How to play
  • Pass three cards (left, right, across, then hold) before each hand.
  • The player with the 2♣ leads the first trick. Follow suit when you can.
  • Hearts and the Queen of Spades are penalty cards (1 each / 13). Hearts may not be led until broken.
  • Take all 26 points to shoot the moon — everyone else scores 26.
  • First player to 100 ends the match; lowest total wins.
