A few things to know

Cap table software.
Your questions, answered.

What is a cap table?

A cap table, short for capitalization table, records who owns equity in a company. It shows shareholders, share classes, securities, and ownership percentages, including how options and other instruments affect fully diluted ownership.

What does cap table software do?

Cap table software helps you maintain ownership records, manage equity grants, track vesting, and calculate dilution as your company raises funding. Cap Table brings these records together with reports, CSV exports, and financing and exit scenarios.

Is this cap table software free?

Yes. Cap Table is free cap table software for tracking ownership, managing equity, and modeling fundraising. Sign in with a Puter account to save your company’s records in your own Puter cloud.

Can I import a cap table from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Import stakeholders and securities from CSV, map your columns, and preview the changes before importing. You can also restore a Cap Table JSON backup.

What types of equity can I track?

Track common and preferred shares, options, RSUs, warrants, SAFEs, and convertible notes, along with vesting schedules, grants, and exercises.

Where is my data stored?

Your company records and document attachments are stored in your own Puter cloud. You can export reports as CSV and your company data as a JSON backup.

Can I explore before adding my company?

Yes. After signing in, choose sample data during setup to explore ownership, grants, and funding scenarios before adding your own records.
